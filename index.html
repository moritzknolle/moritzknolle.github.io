About

I am a doctoral researcher at the Chair for AI in Healthcare and Medicine at the Technical University of Munich, supervised by Daniel Rueckert, and affiliated with the TUM University Hospital and the Konrad Zuse School of Excellence in Reliable AI.

Before Munich I read Computer Science at the University of Sussex and did postgraduate work at Imperial College London, with a research stay at TUM on machine learning for biomedical imaging in between.

Research

Individual privacy risk

Privacy guarantees are usually stated for a population. I measure what a deployed medical model actually leaks about one patient — and why that risk is distributed so unevenly.

Memorisation & its consequences

What models retain about single training records, how that surfaces at inference time, and how it biases predictions for the people whose data was used.

Trustworthy medical AI

Differentially private training that remains useful in practice, and evaluation that exposes performance and safety gaps before deployment rather than after.
